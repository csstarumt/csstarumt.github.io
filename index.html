Impactful Past Events

CSS Symposium 2025

A forward-looking conference designed to inspire attendees with expert-led talks on AI and digital transformation, alongside a Final Year Project Showcase.

ANT International TRX Open Day

A collaborative initiative empowering students with real-world exposure to the tech industry, including insights into career pathways and technological trends.

R Conference 2025

The largest annual gathering of R users in Malaysia, showcasing the impact of the R programming language in a data-driven landscape.

Github Workshop 2025

An interactive session equipping students with essential skills in version control and collaborative coding to enhance workflow efficiency.

The Google AI Lab

An interactive workshop designed to help students transition from passive AI users to active, critical thinkers, avoiding the learning trap.

My5G Portal Visit

An educational visit helping attendees understand Malaysia's 5G deployment and explore real-world 5G applications across diverse sectors.
